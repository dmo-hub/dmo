Patch Note

15.7.2026 Update
07-15-2026
4,785

Hello, this is the DATs Center.

 

We would like to inform you of the Update and Patch details on 15.07.2026 (Wed).

Please refer to the details below to enjoy the game without inconvenience

 

◎ Update

 

 [New Digimon Added – Apollomon ]

 

 

 

Apollomon has joined the Digital World.

 

[Contents]

 

  • Evolution Tree

 

 

  • Mega : Apollomon

 

  • Grade: U
  • Basic Attribute: (Va)
  • Nature Attribure: (Fire)
  • Affiliated field: DR, Nsp, VB

 

  • Stat Information

 

 

  • Skill Information

 

 

  • Flame Edge (Buff) : Upon activation, your Skill Damage increases by 10% for 8 seconds.
  • Sol Blaster (Buff) : Upon activation, your Skill Damage increases by 18% for 23 seconds
  • Arrow of Apollo (Buff) : Upon activation, your Skill Damage increases by 18% for 23 seconds.

 

  • How to obtain Applomon and complete evolution requirements

 

  • Activate the Evolution Slot using the new item [Heart of the Sun]
  • The new item [Heart of the Sun] can be obtained for a limited time through Data Summon.

 

  • How to obtain Applomon Riding and complete activation requirements

 

  • Open the Riding slot using new riding item [Sol Boost]
  • The new riding item [Sol Boost] can be purchased from Cash Shop.

 

 

[New Deck Added ]

 

2 Types of new deck has been added.

  • The One Who Descended in Radiance

 

 

  • [The One Who Descended in Radiance] Digimon list

 

 

 

  • [The One Who Descended in Radiance] Effect

 

 

  • Radiance Against Despair

 

 

 

  • [Radiance Against Despair] Digimon list

 

 

 

  • [Radiance Against Despair] Effect

 

 

 

[New D-Unit Group Added]

 

 

 

 

 

◎ Event

 

 

#01. [Blazing Pride] Event

 

[Duration]  From after the maintenance on 15.7.2026 ~ until before the maintenance 5.8.2026

 

[Contents]

  1. During the event, NPC<Burning>Meramon will appear at Dats Center.
  2. If you set your partner digimon as [Apollomon], you can accept the [[Sub] Blazing Pride!] from NPC<Burning>Meramon
  3. Talk to <Burning>Meramon to complete the quest.
  4. Upon completing the quest, you will obtain [[Achievement] Blazing Pride] that limited achievement item of [Apollomon].

 

  • Limited Achievement Information

 

 

 

#02. [I'm Burning Too] Event

 

[Duration]  From after the maintenance on 15.7.2026 ~ until before the maintenance 5.8.2026

 

[Contents]

  1. During the event, NPC<Burning>Meramon will appear at Dats Center
  2. You can accept the [[Daily] I'm Burning Too] quest from NPC<Burning>Meramon.
  3. Upon accepting the quest, you can obtain [Ever-Burning Flame] 1ea.
  4. You can summon the <Blazing Pride> Apollomon using the [Ever-Burning Flame] in the D-Terminal Underground Summon Square.
  5. Defeat the <Blazing Pride> Apollomon to complete the quest.
  6. Upon completing the quest, you can obtain [Gift of Flame] 1ea as a reward.

 

  • Random Box Information
