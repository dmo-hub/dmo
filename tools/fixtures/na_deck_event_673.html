FINISHED11.06.2024 Update
06-11-2024
33,245

Hello. This is Dats center.

We would like to inform you about the update on June 11th 2024.
Please refer to the information below to avoid any inconvenience.

◎ Update


[New Digimon Added– Kuzuhamon MikoMode]


Kuzuhamon MikoMode has joined the Digital World.

[Content]

- Evolution Tree



- Super Mega: Kuzuhamon MikoMode

◎ Rank : U
◎ Evolution Level : 70
◎ Base Attribute : Data (Da)
◎ Natural Attribute : Darkness
◎ Field : Nso, Uk

- Stat Information



- Skill Information



- Conditions and Evolution Method to Obtain Kuzuhamon MikoMode

 Open the Kuzuhamon MikoMode evolution slot for Renamon.
 The evolution item [Rite of Purification] for Kuzuhamon MikoMode can be obtained through Data Summon: Ultimate.

- Kuzuhamon MikoMode Riding Mode

 Use the riding item [Blessing of the Shrine Maiden] to open Kuzuhamon MikoMode Riding Mode.
 [Blessing of the Shrine Maiden] can be purchased from the Cash Shop.

[New Deck Added – God’s Will]

◎ [God’s Will] Digimon List



◎ [God’s Will] Effect



[New Deck Added – [Leader of the Awakened Four Holy Beasts]

◎ [Leader of the Awakened Four Holy Beasts] Digimon List



◎ [Leader of the Awakened Four Holy Beasts] Effect



[Ticket-Only Bag]

A new inventory [Ticket-Only Bag] has been added.

[Content]

1. It can be accessed via the [Special Bag] button in the bag menu.
2. The [Ticket-Only Bag] has been added to the list of special bags.
3. Various dungeon entry tickets can be stored in the [Ticket-Only Bag].
4. The default space provided for the [Ticket-Only Bag] is 200 slots, and it cannot be expanded.
5. When entering a dungeon, tickets from the [Ticket-Only Bag] will be used first.
6. The type information displayed in the tooltip for dungeon entry ticket items will be unified as [Dungeon Entry Ticket].

[Enhancement of Existing Macro Protector and Addition of Macro Detection System]

  • The existing macro protector system has been improved.
  • A macro detection system has been added.
     

◎ Event

#01. [Kuzuhamon Shrine Miko Mode Release Celebration Double Event]

[Duration] After the maintenance on 11.06.2024 ~ Before the maintenance on 25.06.2024

[Content]

- Event Content
1. During the event period, the stats HP, DS, DE, and AT of Kuzuhamon MikoMode will be doubled.

◎ Kuzuhamon MikoMode Event Stat Information



2. During the event period, Kuzuhamon MikoMode's skill damage is doubled.

◎ Kuzuhamon MikoMode Event Skill Damage Information



#02. [The One Who Purifies Negativity] Event

[Duration] After the maintenance on 11.06.2024 ~ Before the maintenance on 09.07.2024

[Content]

1. During the event period, NPC <Preparing> Kuzuhamon will appear at [DATS Center].
2. When setting your partner Digimon to Kuzuhamon MikoMode, you can accept the quest [[Sub] The One Who Purifies Negativity]] from NPC <Preparing> Kuzuhamon.
3. Complete the quest by talking to NPC <Preparing> Kuzuhamon.
4. Upon completing the quest, you will receive the exclusive achievement [The One Who Purifies Negativity] for Kuzuhamon MikoMode.

◎ [Achievement] The One Who Purifies Negativity



※ All items given as Event Rewards are non-tradable and marked [Event].

#03. [Appearance! Kuzuhamon MikoMode] Event

[Duration] After the maintenance on 11.06.2024 ~ Before the maintenance on 09.07.2024

[Content]

- How to Participate
1. During the Event period, NPC <Preparing> Kuzuhamon will appear in [DATS Center].
2. Accept the quest [[Daily] Appearance! Kuzuhamon MikoMode] from NPC <Preparing> Kuzuhamon.
3. Upon accepting the quest, receive 1 [Summoning Circle].
4. Use the [Summoning Circle] at [D- Terminal Sub-Summoning Room] in [DATS Center] to summon and defeat Kuzuhamon MikoMode to complete the quest.
5. Complete the quest to receive 1 [Kuzuhamon’s Gift].

◎ [Kuzuhamon’s Gift] Random List



※ All items given as Event Rewards are non-tradable.

#04. [Guilmon’s Seal Exchange!] Event

[Duration] After the maintenance on 11.06.2024 ~ Before the maintenance on 25.06.2024

[Content]
